Application overview

BTC/ETH/SOL Data Observer

A private, single-operator research tool for observing BTC/USD, ETH/USD, and SOL/USD market data on the operator’s Mac.

What the tool does

The tool is designed to request Alpaca market data in the paper environment. It does not place trades, change account settings, or redistribute market data.

What this page does

This page describes the application. It has no sign-in, OAuth callback, data collection form, or published market feed.
